I am delighted to share that I was awarded the [AIchemy Collaborative Travel Fund](https://aichemy.ac.uk/collaborative-travel-fund-2025/) to support my research on "Learning Methods for safe human-in-the-loop robotic experimentation in chemistry labs" in collaboration with the [Edinburgh Centre for AI in Assistive Autonomy](https://assistive-autonomy.ed.ac.uk/) and the [Edinburgh Centre for Robotics](https://www.edinburgh-robotics.org/).

This collaboration will strengthen my work at the interface of AI, robotics, and Human-in-the-loop robotic experimentation in shared laboratories for accelerating scientific discovery, and I am grateful to AIchemy for supporting this research visit.
