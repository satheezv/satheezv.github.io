Delivered an in-person research talk on "Human-Aware and Risk-Aware Robot Behaviors in Shared Laboratories" at the University of Bath.

The talk presented recent work on human-aware robot behavior and proactive risk evaluation for mobile robot chemists operating in shared laboratory environments, drawing on:

- [Human-Aware Robot Behaviour in Self-Driving Labs](/publications/HARB)
- [PREVENT: Proactive Risk Evaluation and Vigilant Execution of Navigation and Manipulation Tasks for Mobile Robot Chemists](/publications/PREVENT)
